Prototype · Problem 2.6

An offline-first early-warning layer for school dropout. It turns the attendance a teacher already takes into one ranked “who needs you this week” list — each with a single, concrete action.

It rides on the APAAR student ID so a child’s risk trail follows them across school transfers and seasonal migration — the once-only, interoperable lesson from Estonia, applied to exactly one record.

  • 01Capture — daily attendance in ~30s, fully offline, on a ₹7k phone.
  • 02Rank — an on-device model watches each child’s own trajectory and surfaces the 3–5 who are slipping.
  • 03Act — one suggested action per child; tap to log it and close the loop.
  • 04Carry — risk history travels with the child via APAAR.
Demo data is synthetic. Toggle the network chip (top-right of the phone) to see offline-first behaviour. Switch EN / हिंदी in the header.
